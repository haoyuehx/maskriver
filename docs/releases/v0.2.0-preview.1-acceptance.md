## 1. Release scope

Supported: M1 SQLite `scan`/`preview`/Ed25519 `approve`/`apply`/`validate`;
M2 `--streaming` with budget and transactional outbox; `init-outbox`,
`init-recovery`, read-only `status`, explicit `--resume`, transactional
checkpoint/receipt/progress, bounded prefix/suffix verification, commit-unknown
reconciliation and idempotent replay.

Not supported: MySQL CLI/Recovery, Windows/macOS streaming or recovery, WAL
recovery, cross-store mapping atomicity, generic distributed exactly-once, real
power-loss durability, production-grade PII accuracy, anonymisation,
collision-free replacement, tamper-proof history, and continuous approval
re-check during a long resume.
